# WealthOS — 12-Month Operational Growth Roadmap
## Month-by-Month Execution Playbook from $0 to $100,000 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

---

### Phase 1: Launch & Founder Acquisition (Months 1 – 3)

#### Month 1: Product Launch & Founder's Deal Campaign
* **Primary Objective**: Acquire first 100 Founder's Deal customers ($29,900 gross revenue) and establish initial customer feedback loop.
* **Key Actions**:
  1. Launch landing page (`wealthos-growth-kit/landing-page.html`) featuring $299 Lifetime Founder's Deal (limited to 500 spots).
  2. Execute Day 1–30 Social Media Content Schedule across LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Facebook.
  3. Publish Short-Form Viral Videos 1–10 on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
  4. Recruit first 25 affiliate partners (finance bloggers & WordPress agencies).
* **Revenue Target**: $29,900 gross ($2,500 MRR equivalent).

#### Month 2: Content Engine Acceleration & Product Refinement
* **Primary Objective**: Scale paid ad testing on Meta & Google Search.
* **Key Actions**:
  1. Test Meta Ad Angles: "Rear-View Mirror Budgeting Trap" vs "Debt Avalanche Calculator".
  2. Implement feedback from initial founder cohort; release version 1.1.0 update.
  3. Publish Short-Form Viral Videos 11–20.
* **Revenue Target**: $45,000 gross revenue.

#### Month 3: Evergreen Funnel Transition & B2B Agency Outreach
* **Primary Objective**: Roll out B2B Agency License ($199/year) targeting financial coaches and web agencies.
* **Key Actions**:
  1. Launch B2B agency cold outreach campaign using direct sales playbook (`wealthos-growth-kit/SALES_PLAN.md`).
  2. Transition B2C funnel to evergreen annual licenses ($79/year).
  3. Publish Short-Form Viral Videos 21–30.
* **Revenue Target**: $15,000 MRR ($180k ARR pace).

---

### Phase 2: Channel Scaling & Affiliate Expansion (Months 4 – 6)

#### Month 4–6 Key Execution Milestones
* **Affiliate Network Expansion**: Onboard 150 active affiliate partners driving 35% of top-of-funnel traffic.
* **SEO Content Hub**: Publish 30 high-ranking comparison and tutorial articles (`WealthOS vs YNAB`, `How to Calculate Debt Avalanche`).
* **Milestone Target (Month 6)**: $35,000 MRR ($420k ARR pace).

---

### Phase 3: Scale & Enterprise Agency Program (Months 7 – 12)

#### Month 7–12 Key Execution Milestones
* **Coaching Platform Integrations**: Build white-label portal integrations for financial coaching firms.
* **Internationalization**: Localize plugin translation files for German, Spanish, and French markets.
* **Milestone Target (Month 12)**: **$100,000 Monthly Recurring Revenue ($1.2M ARR)**.
