### Direct Sales Playbook & Objection Handling

#### Objection 1: "Why shouldn't I just use a free budgeting app like Mint or YNAB?"
* **Sales Response**:
  "Generic budgeting apps operate like rear-view mirrors — they show you where your money went after it's gone. WealthOS is an interconnected financial operating system. If your expenses rise by $150, WealthOS instantly calculates the exact ripple effect on your debt payoff schedule, emergency fund buffer, and 15-year Financial Independence timeline. Plus, your data stays 100% private on your own WordPress site with zero monthly SaaS subscriptions."

#### Objection 2: "Is it difficult to set up on my WordPress site?"
* **Sales Response**:
  "Not at all! Installation takes less than 2 minutes. You simply upload the zip file, click Activate, and place the `[wealthos_dashboard]` shortcode on any page. Our guided Onboarding Setup Wizard takes care of the rest."
