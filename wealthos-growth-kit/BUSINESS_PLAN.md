# WealthOS — Executive Business Plan
## Master Financial Operating System Commercial Strategy & Unit Economics

---

### Executive Summary
**WealthOS** is an integrated, self-hosted Personal Wealth Operating System built for WordPress. Unlike generic budgeting SaaS apps (e.g. YNAB, Mint) that merely track historical expenses, WealthOS acts as an interconnected financial model connecting Income, Cash Flow, Expense Control, Emergency Buffers, Debt Reduction (Avalanche vs. Snowball), Savings, Investments, Productive Assets, Business Growth, and Net Worth Projections.

---

### Market Analysis & Opportunity

#### Market Size
* **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: $4.8B global personal finance & wealth management software market.
* **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**: $810M WordPress plugin & self-hosted web software market (810M+ active WordPress sites worldwide).
* **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**: $12M captured via financial bloggers, wealth coaches, freelancers, and WordPress power users over 36 months.

#### Target Buyer Personas
1. **The Ambitious Wealth Builder**: Wants a single dashboard to calculate net worth, compound growth, and 25x FI targets.
2. **The High-Income Freelancer & Entrepreneur**: Needs to manage irregular revenue, operating expenses, and owner draws.
3. **The Debt-Free Crusader**: Seeks mathematical payoff strategies (Debt Avalanche) to eliminate credit card liabilities.
4. **The Financial Coach / Agency**: Wants to white-label the software under their own brand for coaching clients.

---

### Pricing Strategy & Revenue Model

WealthOS operates on a hybrid Annual License & Lifetime Deal model:

#### 1. Personal License — $79 / year (1 Site)
* Full access to all 11 wealth lifecycle engines.
* Standard updates & customer support.

#### 2. Entrepreneur & Agency License — $199 / year (10 Sites)
* White-label rebranding settings (custom app title, tagline, logo, primary/accent colors).
* Export & client reporting features.

#### 3. Lifetime VIP Founder Deal — $299 one-time (5 Sites)
* Scarcity-driven launch deal limited to first 500 customers.

### Key Competitive Moats
1. **Self-Hosted Privacy Moat**: 100% data ownership on user's own WordPress server. Zero third-party tracking.
2. **Interconnected Financial Model**: Direct ripple-effect calculations (expenses $\rightarrow$ cash flow $\rightarrow$ debt timeline $\rightarrow$ net worth).
3. **White-Label Agency Moat**: Allows financial coaches to rebrand the entire platform for their clients.
