### 1. Product Positioning & Value Propositions

#### Headline Tagline
> **"Your Personal Wealth Operating System."**
> *Stop looking in the rear-view mirror with simple budgeting. Turn your money into an integrated wealth-building system.*

#### Primary Positioning Statement
Most financial software merely acts as an expense tracker — telling you where your money *went* after it's already gone. **WealthOS** is an interconnected financial operating system for WordPress. It connects your income, expenses, cash flow, debt elimination, savings, investments, and business growth into a single intelligent dashboard that tells you exactly **what to do next**.

#### Core Value Pillars
1. **Interconnected Financial Intelligence**: If your expenses rise, WealthOS immediately calculates the ripple effect on your savings rate, debt payoff timeline, and 5-year projected net worth.
2. **The #1 Bottleneck Diagnosis**: Automatically pinpoints the single highest-leverage financial action holding you back right now.
3. **Complete Data Privacy & Ownership**: Your financial data stays on your self-hosted WordPress site. No monthly SaaS fees, no data broker sales, and no mandatory third-party bank linking.

### 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is WealthOS a financial adviser?**
*A: No. WealthOS is an educational financial-planning software tool. It provides calculations and structural planning based on user-entered data, but does not provide fiduciary financial advice or guarantee returns.*

**Q: Where is my financial data stored?**
*A: All data is stored securely in your WordPress site's local MySQL database. WealthOS does not transmit your personal data to external servers.*

**Q: Can I customize the plugin name and branding?**
*A: Yes! WealthOS includes full white-labeling settings under `WordPress Admin -> WealthOS -> Settings`. You can customize the app name, tagline, logo, primary color, accent color, and currency symbol.*
