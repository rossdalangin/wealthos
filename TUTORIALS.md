### Tutorial 1: Beginner Wealth Building — From Zero to Financial Foundation

#### Goal
Take someone who has never systematically tracked money and turn their raw numbers into a clear $1,000$ starter emergency reserve and positive cash-flow surplus.

#### Step 1: Complete Onboarding Profile
1. Navigate to the page containing `[wealthos_dashboard]` or click **WealthOS** in the WordPress admin menu.
2. Fill out the **Welcome & Profile Form**:
   - **Age Range**: Select `25-34` (or your current range).
   - **Currency Symbol**: Enter `$` (or your local symbol e.g., `€`, `£`).
   - **Employment Type**: Select `Employed / Salaried`.
   - **Primary Goal**: Enter `Build Starter Emergency Reserve & Control Cash Flow`.
3. Click **Complete Setup & Enter System**.

#### Step 2: Log All Income Sources
1. Click on the **Income** tab.
2. Under **Add Income Stream**, enter your primary job earnings:
   - **Name**: `Primary Salary`
   - **Category**: `Salary`
   - **Amount**: `4500`
   - **Frequency**: `Monthly`
3. Click **Save Income**.
4. *(Optional)* Log any side hustles or secondary income streams.

#### Step 3: Audit Monthly Expenses
1. Click on the **Expenses** tab.
2. Enter your essential living expenses:
   - Rent/Mortgage: `$1,400` (Essential)
   - Groceries: `$450` (Essential)
   - Utilities & Phone: `$200` (Essential)
3. Enter discretionary expenses:
   - Dining Out: `$350` (Discretionary)
   - Streaming Services: `$65` (Discretionary)
4. Check the **Overview** tab to see your calculated **Cash Flow Surplus**:
   $$\text{Income } (\$4,500) - \text{Expenses } (\$2,465) = \text{Cash Flow Surplus } (\$2,035/\text{month})$$

#### Step 4: Build Stage 1 Starter Emergency Reserve
1. Click on the **Savings** tab.
2. Under **Add Savings Goal**, enter:
   - **Goal Name**: `Starter Emergency Fund`
   - **Target Amount**: `1000`
   - **Current Amount**: `250`
3. Click **Save Goal**.
4. Set an automated monthly transfer of `$250/month` until the fund reaches `$1,000`.

### Tutorial 2: Debt Freedom Masterclass — Avalanche vs. Snowball Step-by-Step

#### Goal
Eliminate high-interest credit card debt using mathematical prioritization.

#### Step 1: Log All Debts
1. Click on the **Debts** tab.
2. Log your balances:
   - **Card A**: Balance: `$2,000`, Interest Rate: `22.0%`, Minimum Payment: `$50`
   - **Card B**: Balance: `$5,000`, Interest Rate: `15.0%`, Minimum Payment: `$100`
   - **Auto Loan**: Balance: `$12,000`, Interest Rate: `5.5%`, Minimum Payment: `$250`

#### Step 2: Choose Your Payoff Strategy
* **Debt Avalanche (Mathematical Choice)**:
  - System sorts debts by highest interest rate first (`Card A (22%)` $\rightarrow$ `Card B (15%)` $\rightarrow$ `Auto Loan (5.5%)`).
  - Saves the maximum amount of money in lifetime interest charges.
* **Debt Snowball (Behavioral Choice)**:
  - System sorts debts by smallest balance first (`Card A ($2k)` $\rightarrow$ `Card B ($5k)` $\rightarrow$ `Auto Loan ($12k)`).
  - Delivers rapid psychological momentum by closing whole accounts faster.

#### Step 3: Allocate Surplus Payoff Acceleration
1. Determine your extra monthly payoff budget from your Cash Flow Surplus (e.g., `$300` extra/month).
2. Pay minimums on Card B (`$100`) and Auto Loan (`$250`).
3. Direct `$50 + $300 = $350` toward Card A until paid off.
4. Once Card A hits `$0`, roll the entire `$350` into Card B (`$350 + $100 = $450/month`).

### Tutorial 3: Entrepreneur & Freelancer Guide — Managing Irregular Income & Business Cash Flow

#### Goal
Separate personal and business finances, manage irregular freelance income, and ensure business cash reserves.

#### Step 1: Log Business Revenue & Expenses
1. Click on the **Business** tab.
2. Enter last month's numbers:
   - **Monthly Revenue**: `$14,000`
   - **Operating Expenses**: `$4,000`
   - **Owner Compensation / Draw**: `$6,000`
3. View your **Operating Profit**:
   $$\text{Revenue } (\$14,000) - \text{Expenses } (\$4,000) = \text{Profit } (\$10,000)$$
   $$\text{Retained Business Reserve } = \text{Profit } (\$10,000) - \text{Owner Draw } (\$6,000) = \$4,000$$

#### Step 2: Use the Business Growth Activity Calculator
1. If your goal is `$20,000` monthly revenue with an average client retainer of `$2,000`:
   $$\text{Required Active Clients} = \frac{\$20,000}{\$2,000} = 10 \text{ Clients}$$
2. If your proposal conversion rate is `25%`:
   $$\text{Required Qualified Sales Opportunities} = \frac{10}{0.25} = 40 \text{ Prospects}$$

### Tutorial 5: Wealth Accelerator — Scaling Productive Assets & Reaching Financial Independence (FI)

#### Goal
Transition from saving to accumulating cash-flowing productive assets and calculating your Financial Independence (FI) timeline.

#### Step 1: Track Productive Assets
1. Click on the **Assets** tab.
2. Log cash-flowing assets:
   - **Duplex Rental Property**: Value: `$350,000`, Monthly Cash Flow: `$850`
   - **Digital Media Site**: Value: `$45,000`, Monthly Cash Flow: `$600`

#### Step 2: Calculate Financial Independence Number (25x Rule)
1. Determine annual essential expenses (e.g., `$48,000/year`).
2. Calculate FI Target:
   $$\text{FI Target} = \$48,000 \times 25 = \$1,200,000$$
3. Use the **Compounding Calculator** to test return assumptions:
   - Initial Investment: `$50,000`
   - Monthly Contribution: `$1,500`
   - Return Rate: `7.0%`
   - Horizon: `20 Years`
   - **Estimated Final Portfolio**: `~$885,000+`
